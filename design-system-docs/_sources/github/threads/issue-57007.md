# Issue #57007: DateTimePicker: Localize the order of month, day and year

- Updated: 2024-07-08T14:29:00Z

## Body

## What problem does this address?

The current `DateTimePicker` displays the date in the order "month, day, year" or "day, month, year", depending on the `is12Hour` prop.

![datetimepicker-en](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/assets/54422211/c502be7a-012e-48ed-bed9-6c1b86c4f81f)

The year is always at the end, but in Asian languages such as Chinese and Japanese, the date is always written in the order of year, month, and day, regardless of whether they use 12-hour notation or not.

Therefore, in the Post Editor, the publish date and the date displayed by the `DateTimePicker` component do not match, causing confusion.

![ja](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/assets/54422211/617923fd-3350-4267-a5e8-d5786a832f02)

## What is your proposed solution?

I'm thinking of making the `DateTimePicker` component consider the language when rendering the year, month, and day. For example, WordPress core seems to control the order of input fields by passing them through [a translation function](https://github.com/WordPress/wordpress-develop/blob/5eef2bbb2a476fc3f94f7d7c2d89ff01b9b74f82/src/wp-admin/includes/template.php#L837-L869).

As shown below, in the post list quick edit panel, you can see that the order of  month, day and year changes depending on the language.
