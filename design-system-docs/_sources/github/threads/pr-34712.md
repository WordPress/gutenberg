Yes, the benefit of box shadows is that you can stack as many of them as you need, to accomplish very complex designs, such as the 1.5px inner stroke with a subtle outer drop shadow.

The one thing we need to keep in mind, though, is to always pair it with a `transparent` border or outline, as box-shadows are removed entirely in Windows High Contrast mode, whereas transparent colors are made opaque. By combining both, the shadow is shown in normal modes, and the transparent outline or border is shown in high contrast modes. There's a little more nuance in [this video](https://make.wordpress.org/design/2020/10/29/design-show-tell-windows-10-high-contrast-mode/).
